"Good coffee isn't loud. It's a small cup, poured with total attention, handed to you by someone who actually cares whether you enjoy it."

Parklane is easy to walk past — and hard to forget once you've found it. A handful of seats, a well-worn machine, and baristas who treat every single shot like it's the only one that matters. No shortcuts, no filler, no rush.
